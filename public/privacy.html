Textile Studio Lab — Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 25, 2026

Textile Studio Lab is a Shopify app operated by Shakass Communication.

This Privacy Policy explains how Textile Studio Lab collects, uses, stores, and protects information when merchants install and use the app on their Shopify store.

1. Information we collect

When a merchant installs and uses Textile Studio Lab, we may collect and process the following types of information:

Merchant and store information

  • Shopify store domain
  • Shop identifier
  • Installation status
  • App access token provided by Shopify
  • Subscription and billing status
  • App configuration settings

Product and order information

  • Product IDs and variant IDs
  • Product customization data
  • Order ID and order reference when needed to associate a customized design with a Shopify order
  • Customer name and email only when required to identify and retrieve the correct production file for a customized order

Design and production information

  • Uploaded images
  • Generated or edited design previews
  • Text, QR codes, artwork, SVG or image elements added to a design
  • Mockup and product selection
  • Transparent PNG production files generated from the customer's customization
  • Technical metadata needed to save, render, and retrieve the design

Technical and security information

  • Server logs
  • IP address and request metadata used for security, abuse prevention, debugging, and rate limiting
  • Error logs related to app functionality

2. How we use information

We use the collected information to:

  • Install and authenticate the app on a Shopify store
  • Provide the product personalization studio
  • Allow shoppers to customize products before purchase
  • Generate design previews and production-ready PNG files
  • Link customized designs to Shopify cart items and orders
  • Help merchants retrieve production files after checkout
  • Provide support and troubleshooting
  • Prevent abuse, excessive usage, spam, and unauthorized access
  • Maintain app security and reliability
  • Manage app billing and subscription status

3. AI image generation

If a merchant or shopper uses AI image generation features, the uploaded image, prompt, or design input may be sent to an AI service provider only for the purpose of generating the requested visual output.

AI generation is optional and is only triggered by user action.

4. Sharing information with third parties

We do not sell personal information.

We may share limited information with service providers that are necessary to operate the app, including:

  • Shopify, for authentication, billing, cart, order, and app functionality
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers used to run the app
  • AI service providers when AI generation is requested
  • Technical service providers used for storage, security, logging, or support

These providers are used only to deliver the app functionality and maintain the service.

5. Data retention

We retain app data only as long as necessary to provide the service, support merchants, maintain production files, comply with legal obligations, and protect the app from abuse.

Design files and production PNGs may be stored while the app is installed and while they are needed by the merchant for order fulfillment.

When a merchant uninstalls the app or requests deletion, we will delete or anonymize applicable data within a reasonable timeframe, unless retention is required for legal, billing, security, or fraud-prevention purposes.

6. Shopify privacy webhooks

Textile Studio Lab supports Shopify's mandatory privacy webhooks:

  • customers/data_request
  • customers/redact
  • shop/redact

These webhooks are used to respond to privacy and deletion requests according to Shopify's requirements.

7. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including:

  • Secure HTTPS communication
  • Shopify OAuth authentication
  • Server-side validation
  • Access control for protected routes
  • Rate limiting for sensitive actions
  • Secret and token protection
  • Restricted access to production data

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect merchant and customer data from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

8. Merchant responsibilities

Merchants are responsible for informing their customers that customized product data may be processed when they use personalization features on the storefront.

Merchants are also responsible for ensuring that their own privacy policy explains how customer data is collected and processed through their Shopify store.

9. International data transfers

Depending on the hosting and service providers used, data may be processed in countries outside the merchant's or customer's country of residence. We take reasonable steps to ensure that data is handled securely and in accordance with applicable requirements.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
